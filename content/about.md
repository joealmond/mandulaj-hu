I’m József Mandula, a software engineer who enjoys understanding how a system works and making it easier to change.

Most of my work is in TypeScript, with Angular on the frontend, NestJS on the backend, and cloud infrastructure around them. I care about clear responsibilities, useful tests, and automation that makes everyday work more reliable.

I use Obsidian to think in writing: connecting ideas, working through questions, and keeping explanations I want to come back to. This site brings some of those notes into public view. My aim is to leave each reader with something they can try, a distinction that helps, or a clearer way to approach a problem.

For a first look, read [[publishing-notebook-writing|how this notebook gets published]]. You can also find my public code on [GitHub](https://github.com/joealmond).
