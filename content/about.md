I’m József Mandula, a software engineer who likes building practical systems and understanding how their parts fit together.

I work mostly with TypeScript: Angular on the frontend, NestJS on the backend, and cloud infrastructure around them. I care about maintainable architecture, useful automation, and tools that make everyday work calmer and more reliable.

Obsidian is where I think in writing. This website is a deliberately small public view into that notebook—not a complete archive and not a publishing schedule. I will add a note when I believe it is ready to be useful to someone else.
This site will grow slowly, one useful note at a time.

You can find my public code on [GitHub](https://github.com/joealmond).
